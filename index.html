Director of Embodied Intelligence at All3

Robot intelligence, mission control, and fleet-scale autonomy

Updated August 2026

I lead embodied AI at All3 in London, spanning single-robot behaviour and Mission Control System (MCS) capabilities — connecting learning-based intelligence with scalable deployment, fleet management, and plan execution.

Before All3 I was a Principal Engineer at Humanoid, leading Vision-Language-Action model development, and spent 18 years at Intel, most recently as Principal Engineer in Intel AI Labs leading deep reinforcement learning research. My work centres on the durable foundations of embodied intelligence: robust evaluation, system integration, technical direction, and the path from research prototype to dependable real-world deployment.

Selected work

  • Goal-Conditioned Imitation Learning NeurIPS 2019 · with Pieter Abbeel, UC Berkeley
  • Language-Conditioned Imitation Learning for Robot Manipulation Tasks NeurIPS 2020 · Spotlight · with Heni Ben Amor, Arizona State University
  • MOTO: Offline Pre-training to Online Fine-tuning for Model-based Robot Learning CoRL 2023 · with Chelsea Finn, Stanford University
  • FloorSet: a VLSI floorplanning dataset with design constraints of real-world SoCs IEEE/ACM ICCAD 2024 · AI for electronic design automation
  • Real-time offset adjustment of semiconductor die placement US 12,557,594 · granted February 2026 · most recent of 9 granted US patents
