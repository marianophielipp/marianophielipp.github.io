About Mariano Phielipp

Director of Embodied Intelligence at All3 | PhD in Computer Science

Updated August 2026 Mariano Phielipp

Professional Overview

Since January 2026, I have served as Director of Embodied Intelligence at All3 in London, UK. I lead embodied AI initiatives spanning both single-robot behavior and Mission Control System (MCS) capabilities — connecting learning-based intelligence with scalable deployment, fleet management, and plan execution. My work focuses on building durable foundations for embodied intelligence across evaluation, integration, and long-term technical direction.

Previously, as a Principal Engineer at Humanoid, I led the development of large-scale Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, advancing multimodal autonomy that integrates perception, language, and action in physical environments.

With 18+ years of experience in AI and machine learning, I am highly proficient in Reinforcement Learning, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. I am known for leading initiatives in robotic learning, computer vision, face recognition and detection, object categorization, and recommendation systems. I am recognized for pioneering solutions in online learning, automatic rule learning, natural language processing, knowledge representation, and energy-based algorithms.
